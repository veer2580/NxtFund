DUE DILIGENCE

Built on Evidence.
Backed by Diligence.

We evaluate startups across business, market, product, technology,
financials, legal structure and founder capability before moving
toward an investment decision.

We evaluate startups across 7 key areas
01

Founder & Team

  • Founder background & experience
  • Founder–market fit
  • Full-time commitment
  • Team structure & key roles
  • Previous ventures / track record
  • References
02

Business & Market

  • Problem & solution
  • Market size – TAM / SAM / SOM
  • Customer segment
  • Competitive landscape
  • Business model
  • Revenue model
  • Scalability
03

Product & Technology

  • Product / MVP validation
  • Technology architecture
  • IP ownership
  • AI / data usage (if applicable)
  • Product roadmap
  • Cybersecurity & data practices
  • Technical dependencies
04

Traction & Customers

  • Revenue & growth
  • User / customer numbers
  • Retention
  • Engagement
  • Customer concentration
  • Partnerships
  • Key contracts
05

Financial Due Diligence

  • Historical financial statements
  • Bank / transaction evidence
  • Revenue quality
  • Burn rate
  • Runway
  • Unit economics
  • Cap table
  • Existing debt
  • Previous funding
06

Legal & Compliance

  • Company incorporation
  • Shareholding
  • Founder agreements
  • ESOPs
  • Material contracts
  • Litigation / disputes
  • Regulatory requirements
  • IP / trademark ownership
  • Tax / GST compliance
07

Investment Readiness

  • Funding requirement
  • Proposed use of funds
  • Valuation / investment terms
  • Existing investors
  • Dilution
  • Milestones after investment
  • Exit / liquidity considerations
