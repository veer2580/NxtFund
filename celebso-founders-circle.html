Hosted by Celebso, the Celebso Founders Circle brings together a carefully curated community of verified founders, investors, business leaders and ecosystem professionals for meaningful conversations, strategic networking and potential collaborations.

Designed as an intimate, high-quality gathering rather than a mass networking event, the Founders Circle focuses on creating the right room — bringing together people with ambitious ideas, real businesses, meaningful experience and a shared interest in building what comes next. Every profile is reviewed and verified to help maintain the quality, relevance and exclusivity of the community.

The evening provides an opportunity to step away from formal meetings and crowded networking environments and engage in genuine conversations with people who are actively building, investing, scaling and creating opportunities across industries.

Whether you're building your next venture, exploring strategic capital, looking for potential partners, entering new markets, seeking talent and expertise, or simply want to connect with people who are building at scale — this evening is designed to create conversations that can lead to meaningful relationships and long-term opportunities.

From founders exchanging ideas and experiences to investors discovering emerging businesses, the Celebso Founders Circle is built around one simple principle: the right people in the right room can create possibilities that go far beyond a single evening.

Join an exclusive circle of ambitious individuals shaping businesses, industries and the next generation of entrepreneurship.
