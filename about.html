WHY WE EXIST

Why We Built NXTFUND

We started building while we were still in college. We experienced firsthand how difficult it can be for ambitious founders to turn an idea into a real business without the right capital, resources and ecosystem around them.

We believe no ambitious founder should have to give up on a meaningful idea simply because they could not access the right opportunity.

“

Builders should build. NXTFUND should help open the doors.

”

Our ambition is to help exceptional founders — from India and around the world — turn meaningful ideas into enduring companies.
