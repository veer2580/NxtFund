Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about NXTFund.

Still have questions? Contact us
What is NXTFund?

NXTFund is an early-stage startup fund by Celebso Group. We invest up to ₹10 Crore, four times a year, in a select group of startups, providing funding, incubation, mentorship and an extensive investor network.

What kind of startups does NXTFund invest in?

We invest in early-stage technology startups across sectors — SaaS, fintech, healthtech, agritech, consumer and more. We look for exceptional founders building ambitious, category-defining companies.

Who can apply for funding?

Any early-stage founder building a technology-driven startup in India can apply. We welcome first-time founders, experienced entrepreneurs and teams from across the country.

Do you take equity in startups?

Yes, in exchange for funding we take a small equity stake in the startup. This aligns our interests and allows us to support your long-term growth and success.

What is the funding range?

We invest up to ₹10 Crore per batch, distributed across a select group of startups. The exact amount per startup depends on the stage, team and opportunity.

Is there an application fee?

No, applying to NXTFund is completely free. We never charge founders an application fee. The entire application and selection process is free of cost.

How does the selection process work?

Applications are reviewed by our team in stages — initial screening, video submission, founder meetings, and finally a pitch to our investor audience. Selected founders get funded and incubated at Celebso Startup Valley.

How can I track my application?

After applying, you will receive email updates at each stage of the process. You can also contact us at Startup.nxtfund@gmail.com for any updates regarding your application.
